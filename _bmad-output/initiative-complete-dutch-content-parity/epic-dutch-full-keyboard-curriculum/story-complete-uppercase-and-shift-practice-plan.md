**Approach:** Implement `nl-shift-02` through `nl-shift-09` from `full-keyboard-curriculum-outline.md`, with short reach introductions, Dutch retrieval, mixed drills and a review. Each lesson uses the mapped lowercase key and opposite-hand Shift.
